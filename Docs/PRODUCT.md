# AirStrum Product Notes

## English

AirStrum turns a paired iPhone and Apple Watch into a playful air-guitar
instrument. The defining gesture is a two-hand split: one hand holds a chord
on the phone; the other hand strums with the watch.

The first release prioritizes the short loop from physical gesture to coherent
sound. It does not attempt to be a guitar teaching tool, a song library, a
radio app, or a project-management companion.

## 中文

AirStrum 将配对的 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 变成一件好玩的空气吉他乐器。核心
动作是双手分工：一只手在手机上按住和弦，另一只手通过手表扫弦。

第一版优先保证从身体动作到连贯声音的短反馈链路。不把自己定义成吉他教学
工具、歌曲库、Radio 应用或项目管理工具。
